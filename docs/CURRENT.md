## What you can do

| Input | Effect | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| A / D | Roll left / right: the roll rate builds toward 20 deg/s (tunable) with a 0.5 s lag, so a tap is a small bank and a hold a full roll, to at most 60 deg. A released bank stays (no self-leveling) | `input/keyState.ts`, `sim/flight/attitude.ts`, `config/motion.ts` |
| Space | In the air: wings level, one press rolls the bank to exactly 0 and stops (latched, not hold; A or D cancels it). **On the ground (C5): wheel brakes while held.** A press does both and the sim uses whichever applies; a pause releases the brake | `input/keyState.ts`, `sim/flight/attitude.ts` |
| `[` / `]` | Heading bug left / right 5 deg (Shift: 1 deg), repeats while held | `input/keyboard.ts`, `views/headingBug.ts` |
| `-` / `=` | Altitude bug down / up 100 ft (Shift: 10 ft), repeats while held | `input/keyboard.ts`, `views/altitudeBug.ts` |
| F / R | Flaps one step out / in (up, 1 takeoff, 2 landing), one press one command; allowed on the ground, refused after a crash. Full travel 6 s | `input/keyState.ts`, `sim/flight/wing.ts` |
| G | Gear up / down, one press one command. Refused on the ground and after a crash. Travels in 3 s | `input/keyState.ts`, `sim/flight/gear.ts` |
| S / W on the ground | Raise / lower the nose once fast enough (no authority below 35 m/s equivalent; held to 0..11 deg) | `sim/flight/ground.ts` |
| A / D on the ground | Steer: 15 m radius when slow, at most 20 deg/s, 2.9 deg/s at 60 m/s | `sim/flight/ground.ts` |
| Q / E | Move the throttle lever down / up, 50% per s (tunable); it stays where left. The engine follows with a lag (1.2 s time constant, much longer spooling up from near idle; below) | same |
| S / W | Nose up / down. A tap is a small nudge (about 0.1 deg); the pull ramps up with hold time, from 12% of the rate to full (8 deg/s, tunable) after 1.2 s, and the rate follows the key through a 0.3 s lag. The nose stays where released. Raising it trades speed for height; too much angle of attack stalls | same |
| Backquote (`) | Show / hide the debug panel (over the comms terminal), running or paused | `input/keyboard.ts`, `app/debugPanel.ts` |
| P | Pause / resume (also pauses on window blur or hidden tab, and stays paused on return) | `input/keyboard.ts`, `app/pause.ts` |
| C | Recenter the top-view camera on the plane | `main.ts`, `views/followCamera.ts` |
| Enter | After a crash, or after landing and stopping (under 0.5 m/s with a touchdown on record), restart at the start position. Ignored otherwise (in flight, or at the runway start), and ignored on a focused button | `input/keyboard.ts` (`actionFor`), `sim/step.ts` |
| Top-view click | Place the plane there, at least 20 m above ground | `input/mapPointer.ts` |
| Top-view drag / wheel | Pan (leaves follow mode) / zoom | `input/mapPointer.ts`, `views/followCamera.ts` |
| HSI click | Open or close the runway menu (below); `Escape` or `X` also closes it | `app/runwayMenu.ts` |
| Side-view drag / wheel | Rotate heading / zoom | `input/sidePointer.ts`, `views/sideCamera.ts` |

The top-view wheel edits the zoom of the current phase only (ground, approach or air), the side-view wheel that of the current height band (below).

Held keys steer; keys are physical positions (`event.code`). Text fields never steer; range sliders, buttons and checkboxes are not text fields (`isTextEntry`), so flight keys still work after touching a slider.

## Simulation (`src/sim/`, headless)

- **State** (`state.ts`): `tick`, `craft` {x, y, z, heading, speed, pathAngle, pitch, pitchRate, bank, rollRate, lever, spool, onGround}, `controls` {turn, throttle, pitch, level, brake}, `crash` {tick, speed, pathAngle, `cause`, `sink`}, `tuning`, `start` (a `StartSpec`: where `restart` goes), `liftoffTick` and `touchdown` (C5: the last survived touchdown {tick, x, y, heading, sink m/s down, speed, bank, pitch, kind}; cleared by restart and start). `craft` also has `flapLever` (0, 1, 2) and `flaps` (0..1, 6 s full travel, `flight/wing.ts`), and `gearDown` (lever) and `gear` (0 up .. 1 down and locked, 3 s travel, `flight/gear.ts`); the runway start has the gear down, the air start up. All `readonly`. `pitch` (nose) and `pathAngle` (direction of travel) are separate; angle of attack is `pitch - pathAngle`. The page starts on the ground by default; `?start=final` (e.g. `http://localhost:5173/?start=final`) opens on the Final start instead (1,400 m out, on the center line and the 3 deg glide path, gear down) and `?start=air` on the air start; the debug panel buttons do the same live. The runway start (`runwayStart` in `sim/world/starts.ts`): runway 07, 40 m past the threshold, at rest, engine idle. The old air start is `AIR_START` in `state.ts` (debug panel button Air): (4560, 4560), z 200 m, heading 45 deg, 90 m/s, pitch 5.38 deg, lever and engine 32% (level trim; a test recomputes it). These are in `config/motion.ts`.
- **Step** (`step.ts`): `applyCommands` then `advance`, one 1/60 s step each. `advance` runs the ground step (`flight/ground.ts`) when `craft.onGround`, else the air step (`flight/air.ts`). Air order: lever and engine, bank and turn help, pitch, forces (new pitch and bank, old path), path angle (clamped to +/-80 deg), soft ceiling, heading, speed (using the new path), then x/y/z (average speed, so energy is conserved exactly with no engine and no drag below separation). Position is clamped to the map; z is capped at the 2,438.4 m ceiling (40,000 ft).
- **Ground** (`flight/ground.ts`, C4): on the wheels, speed changes by ground-multiplied thrust (`groundMult`, 1.5, thrust only) minus drag minus rolling friction (0.02 paved, 0.12 grass, times the weight on the wheels); a stopped plane stays stopped. Lift unloads the wheels. Liftoff when lift plus thrust-up exceeds 1.01 x weight (rotate at about 62 m/s: lifts off near 68 m/s after about 415 m); the craft is raised 0.05 m. Bank eases to level; wings level does nothing. Rough ground under the wheels (past the end of the runway and its 76 m of grass) is a crash. **Brakes and spoilers (C5):** Space held adds friction 0.40 paved (`brakePct` slider) or 0.20 on grass (scaled by brakePct / 40), times the weight on the wheels. Spoilers are automatic: on the wheels with the lever at or below 10%, lift x0.3 and drag +0.06, so a flare cannot lift off again; power above 10% stows them. Braked from 66 m/s the plane stops in about 350 to 550 m. Stall state is always none on the ground. **Gear (C4B):** `gear` adds `gearDragCounts` (200, slider) times its position to zero-lift drag, in the air and on the wheels; gear up adds nothing, so the air start flies as before. **Touchdown check (C5, `touchdownCheck` in `ground.ts`, called from `flight/air.ts`):** contact from the air is checked in order: `terrain` (rough ground, water, slopes), `gear` (not fully down and locked), `hard` (sink over `touchdownSinkMs`, 5 m/s, slider), `bank` (over 15 deg), `nose` (pitch under -3 deg), `tail` (over 16 deg). Otherwise it is a landing: on the wheels, speed x cos(path), path 0, pitch clamped to 0..16 deg, heading and bank kept (the bank eases out), `touchdown` recorded. No bounce. A skip (touchdown, liftoff, touchdown within 5 s) keeps the larger sink. Grass inside the airport is survivable and reported as off the runway; running off the end is a crash, cause `overrun`. `SimWorld.groundKind` (`world/groundKind.ts`): paved (runway, taxiway, apron), grass (other flat airport ground), rough (all else, always a crash).
- **Commands** (`commands.ts`) are the only way in: `turn` (roll, and steering on the ground; a non-zero value cancels wings level), `level` (ignored on the ground), `brake` (`{value: boolean}`, Space held), `gear` (toggle; refused on the ground and when crashed), `start` (debug panel: restart from a spec, which `restart` then reuses), `throttle`, `pitch`, `restart` (crashed, or landed and stopped), `tune`, and the pointer/test tools `place` (always ends airborne; from the ground it also takes the air start speed and trim), `rotate`. `place` and `rotate` are ignored while crashed. Previously: `throttle`, `pitch`, `restart`, `tune`, and the pointer/test tools `place`, `rotate`. `place` and `rotate` are ignored while crashed and keep pitch and bank.
- **Tuning** (`config/motion.ts`): roll rate, turn multiplier, turn help limit, pitch rate, stall angle, stall nose drop, lever rate, engine spool, spool lag at idle, max thrust, mass, parasite and induced drag. `tune` clamps to the range, ignores unknown keys and non-finite values, works while crashed, and survives restart. A page reload resets it.
- **Buildings are solid** (E5.6, `world/obstacles.ts`): touching a roof or wall is a crash, cause `building`, and the wreck stays at the hit height; the banner says `hit a building`. The terrain warning counts roofs. A top-view click places the plane above them. The plane is a point.
- **Crash** (`contact.ts`): contact is `z <= heightAt(x, y)`, checked along the step in pieces of at most 0.5 m. On contact the craft stops on the ground, pitch and bank freeze, `pitchRate` and `rollRate` become 0, wings level is cleared, `crash` is set, and only `tick` advances. Contact from the air first runs the touchdown check (Ground, above); only a failed check is a crash.
- **Aerodynamics** (`sim/flight/aero.ts`, constants in `config/flight.ts`): `Cl` and `Cd` from angle of attack, smoothly blending a linear polar into a flat plate across the stall (center `stallDeg`, 5 deg wide; negative stall at -13 deg). `stallState` gives `none` / `warning` (3 deg before the center) / `stalled` (separation 0.4). `liftAngle` gives the angle for a load factor.
- **Forces** (`sim/flight/forces.ts`): `forcesOn` gives thrust, lift, drag, load factor, dV/dt, dPath/dt and dHeading/dt; the step and the debug panel both use it. Density falls with real height (`altitudeScale`), thrust falls as density^0.7. Speed is clamped to 20..300 m/s as a safety net.
- **Attitude** (`sim/flight/attitude.ts`): S/W command a pitch rate (0.15 s ramp); the nose holds where released and the path settles toward it. Authority falls below about 55 m/s equivalent airspeed. While separated the nose drops by itself and nose-up stops working. A/D command a roll rate that follows through a 0.5 s lag (`ROLL_LAG_S`); a released bank holds at any size. Space (wings level) rolls toward zero bank at a rate of 1 per second per radian of bank (`LEVEL_GAIN`), capped at the roll rate, and stops at exactly 0 without crossing it. Turn help adds up to 4 deg of nose-up with the bank, to 30 deg of bank. Heading changes by `turnMult x lift x sin(bank)`; `turnMult` never changes energy.
- **Soft ceiling**: the path is turned level as z nears 2,438.4 m (nose lowered at most 20 deg/s); no energy added or removed.
- **World** reaches the sim as `SimWorld` (`width`, `height`, `heightAt`, `altitudeScale`). Real map code lives in `sim/world/`.
- **V-speeds** (`flight/vspeeds.ts`): indicated stall speed from mass and the wing (the one the debug panel shows), VR = 1.05 x Vs, V2 = 1.2 x Vs (`config/flight.ts`). V1 (C5) is accelerate-stop: the highest speed up to VR from which full power to that speed, then idle with brakes and spoilers, stops inside the runway left (`accelerateStopM`, 1 m/s steps at sea level; the engine is taken at full power from rest, so it runs a little optimistic). The strip passes the runway remaining and shows `--` off the runway. `flight/trim.ts` `trimAt` gives the pitch and lever for steady flight at a speed and path; the Final start (`finalStart` in `world/starts.ts`) uses it: 1,400 m before the 07 threshold, 3 deg path aimed 150 m past it, 69 m/s (C6), gear down, full flaps (debug panel button Final).
- **Flaps (C6, `flight/wing.ts`)**: `wingAt(flaps, gear, tuning)` gives Cl0 (0.25 + flaps x `flapLiftPct`/100, 0.60), the stall angle (down 1.5 deg at full flaps) and extra zero-lift drag (flaps x `flapDragCounts` 500 + gear x `gearDragCounts`); every aero caller uses it. Level stall, indicated: 60.6 / 56.5 / 53.1 m/s for flaps up / 1 / 2. In the air the nose is compensated as the flaps move (0.8 x the Cl0 change / lift slope), so extending full flaps at 75 to 90 m/s changes height by under 10 m. No compensation on the ground, no flap speed limit or damage. V-speeds take the current flaps. Flaps 0 with gear up is bit for bit the old wing. **Gear warning:** `GEAR` amber in the warning cell when airborne, under 60 m above ground, under 85 m/s indicated, gear not down and descending; a stall warning wins the cell (`gearWarning` in `flight/gear.ts`).
- **Simplifications, on purpose:** no wind, no spins or structural damage. Trim changes with mass, so the mass slider makes the plane climb or sink.

## App loop (`src/app/`)

- `loop.ts`: fixed 60 Hz stepper (`stepper.ts`, at most 5 steps per frame, extra time dropped). Keys are sampled only on frames that step. The loop owns the only copy of `SimState`; views get an interpolated `snapshot()` between the last two steps.
- Pause drops queued commands and clears held keys. While paused, `send` applies commands immediately and redraws. `tune` is applied at once even while running. `onFrame(elapsedS)` reports real frame intervals (not the first frame or the first after a resume).
- `crashBanner.ts`: `CRASHED: <what>, <altitude> ft - Enter to restart` for terrain (feet as `ALT`); with another cause, `CRASHED: runway 07/25 TST, hard landing 1,240 ft/min - Enter to restart` (sink is unscaled ft/min, like `V/S`). Once landed and stopped, a green `LANDED: runway 07 TST, 380 ft/min, 210 m past threshold, 3 m left - Enter to restart` (off the runway: `LANDED: grass, off runway, ...`); it hides when the plane rolls above 2 m/s. The runway end comes from the heading at touchdown.
- `debugPanel.ts`: Runway and Air start buttons (`start` command), a ground line (on the ground and the surface, or airborne), sliders (now including Ground thrust) for each `Tuning` value (read back from `loop.state.tuning`), Reset, flight state (pitch, path, angle of attack, bank, roll rate, turn rate, level stall speed at this bank, stall state and separation, lift and load factor, true and indicated speed, lever, engine, thrust, drag, weight, dV/dt, density, energy height), scales, and frame timing over the last 300 frames (`frameStats.ts`; interval, top, side and total draw). Timing records while the panel is hidden. Text refreshes at 4 Hz.

## Views (`src/views/`, read-only)

- **Zoom (C4B)**: both views have a 0.5 m/pixel close level. Each remembers a ground zoom (close) and an air zoom (top 5 m/px = 4x); `views/zoomGate.ts` picks the phase (ground on the wheels or under 30.5 m (500 ft) above ground, air over 40 m, last phase between). **The side view uses three height bands instead**, in displayed feet above ground: near (5x, 0.5 m/px) on the wheels or low, mid (4x, 1.25 m/px) and high (3x, 2.5 m/px). Climbing, it leaves near above 1,400 ft and mid above 3,500 ft; descending, it enters mid under 3,000 ft and near under 1,200 ft. While approach guidance is on it ignores the bands and stays on the high band's zoom until the ground phase, then the near one, so the runway stays in sight. Each band remembers its own wheel zoom. In both views the shown zoom eases toward the target in log space (0.8 s). `main.ts` owns this state; paused, the zoom snaps. Fractional zoom is interpolated in `camera.ts` and `sideCamera.ts`; the airport patch is baked at the nearest level and stretched mid-ease.
- **Approach zoom (top view):** with a runway picked, a third zoom (6x) applies while lining up on it: airborne, under 400 world m above ground, before the threshold within 6 km, within 400 m of the center line and 60 deg of its heading (kept out to 600 m, 90 deg, 6.5 km and over the runway). A takeoff from that runway does not start it. The wheel changes and remembers it. Reaching the ground phase from the air always sets 7x (`liningUp`, `topPhase` in `views/zoomGate.ts`).
- **Zoom readout**: each view draws `ZOOM 4x` above its scale bar (`zoomLabel` in `views/camera.ts`). The number is the level, 1x = farthest out, not a magnification: top 1x..7x, side 1x..5x, so the same number is not the same scale in both. While easing it shows the nearest level.
- **Takeoff HUD mode (C4B)**: on the ground, or under 150 m above ground with the gear not up (`takeoffMode` in `views/takeoffStrip.ts`). The side view's heading bug and the altitude bug and tape are hidden, their values kept (the top view's bug shows whenever airborne). The side view shows only `RWY 1,010 m` left ahead; the speeds and call-outs are in the instruments panel (D5A, below). Speeds update while at rest, freeze in the roll and clear when the mode ends. All of it is view state rebuilt each frame in `main.ts`.
- **Top view**: terrain, airport overlay, plane marker, a dashed turn line (as long on screen as the compass ring's radius at any zoom, so it ends at the heading bug; at the current speed and turn rate, tick every 2 s of flight; `views/turnLine.ts`, tuned in `config/display.ts`), and, while following, a compass ring around the plane (turns with the map, ticks every 10 deg, labels every 30) with the heading bug on it, a faint line to the bug, a `BUG 270  L 45` text line, and a bar across the turn line where it reaches the bug. The bug is view state in `main.ts` (arithmetic in `views/headingBug.ts`), starts at the plane's heading, and is not saved with the sim. The ring and bug are hidden in free look and on the ground; on the ground the turn line shows only above 5 m/s and uses the steering rate. Edge arrow when the plane is off screen. Camera follows the plane and rotates so its heading is always up (`followCamera.ts`, `camera.ts`); a north arrow in the corner shows where north is. Panning (free look) keeps the orientation it had.
- **Top view depth cues (C3D)**: a black plane shadow falls away from the sun (west to north to east through the day) in the world (turns with the map) and separates from the plane with height above ground (0.2 px per world m, cap 40 px), fading out between 200 and 500 m AGL, none after a crash; a pale haze veil over the terrain (not airports) rises to 35% alpha from 1,200 m up; the plane sprite steps through 4 sizes up to 1.4 times at 1,200 m (`views/depth.ts`, size tiers in `views/sprite.ts`). All constants and the `DEPTH_FX` switches are in `config/display.ts`. Clouds (built but OFF: `DEPTH_FX.clouds` is false in `config/display.ts` until they are reworked): a static layer at 500 m (world) drawn over the terrain and airports from one seamless noise tile (`views/cloudTile.ts`, 3000 m per tile), scaled about the view center by their height so they slide past the ground as you climb; faint (alpha 0.25) while under the base so low flight stays clear, 0.6 above it, with soft cloud shadows on the ground, away from the sun, that fade the same way. Banded terrain parallax was tried and shelved (`docs/C3D_PLAN.md`).
- **Approach guidance (C5)**: `views/approach.ts`. On when airborne, gear down, under 150 m above ground, within 4 km before a threshold (or over the runway and not climbing), within 60 deg of the runway heading and 2.5 km of its line; either runway end, the one the plane faces. It replaces the bugs (except the top view's heading bug) and takeoff mode. Top view: the runway center line (see Runway selection). Side view: a dashed green 3 deg glide path ending in a dot at the aim point (150 m past the threshold), and at top center `LOC 40 m L   HDG +3   GS 8 m HIGH` (world meters; LOC is the plane's side of the line, HDG the plane's heading minus the runway's). The same path and aim point as the Final start. An off-screen-runway arrow was not built.
- **Side view**: the plane is 18% of the width from the left. `AGL 120 ft` (displayed feet, like `ALT`) by the plumb line under 60 m (2,000 ft) while airborne; a 6 m flare reads `AGL 200 ft`. Terrain profile along the heading, plane sprite (with wheels; on the ground it is lifted 7 px, fading to none by 40 px of height, `wheelOffsetPx` in `sideCamera.ts`; the plumb and path lines start from the drawn plane), plane sprite at the nose pitch (25 frames, -30 to +30 deg, wheels drawn when the gear is past half), and a short dim line from the plane along its path angle (the gap is the angle of attack; hidden when crashed), wreck sprite after a crash. Scrolls vertically only when the plane climbs above about 40% of panel height (`sideCamera.ts`). At the closest zoom above about 400 m no ground is visible; zoom out.
  - **Altitude bug**: a cyan dashed line across the view at the bug altitude (displayed feet, same as `ALT`), a short bar on it where the current path line reaches it, and `^` / `v ALT BUG` text when it is off screen. On the right, an altitude tape magnified far past the main scale (0.4 px per ft, so 10 ft is 4 px; about +/-300 ft): ticks every 10 / 50 / 100 ft, the plane's altitude at the middle (yellow pointer), the bug as a cyan bar (clamped to the edge when far off), a yellow trend bar for where altitude will be in 10 s at the current climb rate, and the feet still to climb (`+400 ft`) underneath. The bug is view state in `main.ts`, starts at the plane's altitude to the nearest 100 ft, is kept to 10 ft steps within 0..40000 ft, and is hidden after a crash. Arithmetic in `views/altitudeBug.ts`, constants `ALT_*` in `config/display.ts`.
  - **Slope shading (C3E)**: terrain columns are lighter where the ground rises to the right and darker where it falls (light from the left), strongest on the skin and snow, weaker on the body; flat ground and water are unchanged. `views/sideLayers.ts` (`slopeShade`), constants `SLOPE_*` in `config/display.ts`, switch `SIDE_FX.slopeShade`.
  - **Background layers** come from `SIDE_LAYERS` (`config/display.ts`). Parallax (`sliceWindow` factor `k`, on) makes far layers scroll slower; an optional (off by default) dark, 85%-alpha foreground (`SIDE_FOREGROUND`, 250 to 120 m to the viewer's side, k = 1.3) is drawn over the plane and the plumb line, with a faint ghost of the plane and path line on top when the foreground rises over them (`SIDE_FX.foreground`, and the Side foreground checkbox in the debug panel); it is not part of the readout or collision. Lane bands (`envelope`, on) draw each layer as the highest point over a strip of 3 to 4 parallel lines, so ridges stop popping in and out. Layers are not at their true distance ahead once parallax is on; the cursor readout stays on the main profile.
- Sprites: `views/sprite.ts`, shapes in `config/planeShape.ts`. Colors in `config/palette.ts`.
- **Instruments panel** (`views/instruments.ts`, units in `sim/units.ts`, text in `views/format.ts`): 3 x 4 cells (C6: `FLP` `UP`, `1`, `2`, `>` while moving; the warning cell is on the last row): `SPD` kt (indicated airspeed), `ALT`, `V/S`, `HDG`, `PIT` deg (signed), `BNK` (`L 30`, `0`, `R 30`), `THR` and `ENG` % (D3 replaces the text), `BRK` (on the ground: `ON` while braking, `SPL` while rolling with spoilers out), a warning cell, and `GEAR` (`UP`, `DN`, `--` while moving) on the last row: `STALL` amber (warning) or in a red box (stalled), none when crashed;  `ALT` ft (displayed, nearest 10), `HDG`, `V/S` ft/min (nearest 10, unscaled, so it disagrees with how fast `ALT` changes; D2 decides). Redrawn only when a string changes. A hidden line gives screen readers the values at most 4 times a second. Map and scale ticks in the views stay in meters.

## The map

- **Mid-Atlantic (E3, the default):** `public/maps/mid-atlantic.bin`, written by `node tools/build-map.mjs` (data in `data/map/`, sources in `data/map/SOURCES.md`). A 20 m base grid, 15.58 x 15.10 km, with 10 m patches over both airports and both city cores. Read by `sim/world/mapFile.ts`; lookups use a patch where one covers the point, ground blended to the base over 80 m at its edge (`config/map.ts`). The page shows a loading cover until the map is read and the views are warmed (about 3.5 s of warm-up).
- **City districts (E5.1):** DC and Philadelphia ground is painted `downtown`, `residential`, `suburb`, `industrial` or `park` (codes 12 to 16) from hand-drawn outlines in `data/map/districts.json`, never on water or inside an airport outline. The ground itself only sets color and the readout name (highways and buildings are below), and landing on any of them is still a rough-ground crash.
- **Housing density (E5B):** housing outlines have a `density` from 1 (exurb) to 5 (rowhomes). Each level in `data/map/districts.json` (`types.housing`) sets footprint size along and across the streets, lot gap, block, nudge (how far a building may stray in its lot: 1 at level 1, 0 at level 5), fill and height. Densities 1 to 3 paint `suburb` ground with suburb roofs and 6 to 9 m real heights, 4 and 5 `residential` with 8 to 15 m. Downtown has no nudge, so it is a straight grid. A building never reaches into a district listed later in the file. Assigned: both suburb rings 2; Anacostia and Arlington 3; north DC, Georgetown, West Philadelphia and Camden 4; Capitol Hill, north and south Philadelphia 5. Level 5 blocks are 4 homes side by side and 2 deep. Some districts have their own street angle.
- **Highways (E5.2):** real interstates and a few other highways (US Census TIGER/Line, chosen in `data/map/roads.json`), warped with the map, drawn over the ground in the top view as marked asphalt highways with bridges over water (`views/roadLayer.ts`; markings fade out as you zoom out). Each highway is one line, both directions together. Minor ones are hidden past 10 m/px. They go around airport outlines. Drawing only: no effect on flight or the side view.
- **Buildings (E5.3):** 16,198 rectangles with heights, generated into the districts by `tools/map/buildings.ts` and loaded as `grid.buildings`. Downtown DC (including Southwest) is capped at 42.5 m real (about 140 ft); Rosslyn and Crystal City are 45 to 91 m real (about 150 to 300 ft), set per district in `data/map/districts.json`. Drawn in the top view with shadows from 10 m/px in (`views/cityLayer.ts`, tiles baked per zoom), and in the side view (`views/sideBuildings.ts`): solid where the flight line goes through one, pale within 60 m beside it, hazed skylines farther off.
- **Airports** (`sim/world/midAtlantic.ts`, placed by the map file): DCA runway 01/19, true 002 deg, 1,092 x 23 world m; PHL runway 09R/27L, true 080 deg, 1,600 x 30 world m (widths can be scaled as a practice aid: `RUNWAY_WIDTH_MULT` in `config/map.ts`); one taxiway system and apron each. ## Sun (E8)

- **The day:** 12 min from sunrise to sunset (43,200 steps), then straight back to sunrise; no night and no sky or ground color changes. A game starts mid-morning, 9 min before sunset. Restart and new starts leave the time alone, pause stops it, a page reload resets it. State `SimState.sunClock` (`sim/sun.ts`: path, clock, steps); commands `sunTime` and `sunHold`.
- **Path:** an equinox day at 39 N: east at sunrise (shadows west), south at noon, about 51 deg up (shadows north), west at sunset (shadows east). Mid-morning azimuth 122, mid-afternoon 238.
- **Drawn in 60 steps,** one every 12 s; `main.ts` passes the same `Sun` to both views (`sunForStep`). Shadow length per meter of height is `min(1.5, 0.62 / tan(elevation))` (0.5 at noon, 0.94 mid-morning); `strength` rises from 0 at the horizon to 1 at 10 deg. Constants in `config/sun.ts`.
- `views/sunLight.ts`: the shadow vector, and face brightness `1 + strength x (base + amp x dot(normal, sun) - 1)`, with 0.90 / 0.25 for flat walls and slopes and 0.92 / 0.20 for round walls in the side view.
- **Follows the sun:** building shadows and the darker half (0.72) of pitched roofs; landmark shadows, slopes, flag pole shadows, dome highlight and side-view walls; side-view ordinary buildings (each wall turned to the viewer is lit by the way it faces, two walls at an angle, with a 1 px dark right edge); cloud and bridge shadows (bridge: 7.07 m from the deck). `strength` also fades these shadows. The plane shadow takes only the direction: its length and darkness show height, and its direction jumps at the sunset reset.
- **Building tiles** carry the sun they were painted for. A visible tile that is missing, then one that is out of date, is painted in batches of 100 buildings within 6 ms of a frame, and the old one shows until it is done; so neighbors can differ by a step for a moment. Off-screen tiles wait until seen. A building belongs to every tile its roof or longest shadow (1.5 per m, west through north to east) can reach.
- **Debug panel** SUN (top of the panel): time-of-day slider (1 s a notch), Hold sun, and the azimuth, elevation, drawn step, shadow length and strength.
- Not from the sun: terrain hillshade (northwest) and side-view terrain slope shading.

## Autopilot and stability (D4)

- **Controls:** `Z` turns all three modes on, holding the **present heading, altitude and speed** and moving both bugs there,, or, with any mode on, turns every mode off. Clicking the `HDG`, `ALT` or `SPD` lamp switches that mode alone (`app/apLamps.ts`): HDG and ALT hold the present value and move their bug there, SPD holds the current speed. Setting a bug before engaging does not carry over; engage, then move it. `,` `.` speed target -/+ 5 kt (Shift 1 kt); `[ ]` and `- =` move the bugs and the plane follows. Each flight key drops only its own mode: A, D or Space heading; W or S altitude; Q or E speed. There is no `X` key.
- **Height limit:** engages above 45 world m (about 740 ft) and lets go below 30 (about 490 ft), gear up or down; letting go drops every mode.
- **Stability** (`sim/flight/stability.ts`, pure): two limits per row (engage / let go): ground, height (45 / 30 m; gear not up needs 150 m both), bank 30 / 45, pitch 15 / 25, any stall, speed 1.15 / 1.05 times stall speed, terrain ahead. `advance` judges it once a step, counts `stableTicks` (engage needs 30 = 0.5 s) and lets go on danger.
- **Notices:** a refusal (`AP REFUSED: BANK`) or let-go (`AP OFF: TERRAIN`, red) shows 2 s at the side view's call-out place and in the screen-reader line. The altitude bug line is solid while altitude hold is on.
- **Lamps:** AP green with any mode on; HDG and ALT amber while capturing, green inside 2 deg / 20 ft; SPD likewise within 2 kt, amber while the stall floor (1.25 times stall speed) overrides the target. Cyan speed bug on the airspeed dial. Debug panel FLIGHT text has the autopilot and stability rows.
- **Model:** `sim/flight/autopilot.ts` (control law, bypasses the pitch key ramp via `stepPitch`'s `direct`), commands `autopilot`, `apMode`, `apTarget` (`sim/commands.ts`), state `SimState.autopilot` and `stableTicks`. Constants in `config/flight.ts` (`AP_*`, `STABLE_*`, `DANGER_*`).
- **Bug sync:** the bugs stay view state; every drawn frame `main.ts` sends `apTarget` if the sim's targets differ (`syncAutopilotBugs`).
- **Known:** the AP lets go at 150 m with the gear down, so landings are by hand; a forgotten bug makes the plane turn when `Z` is pressed.

## Takeoff cues and power dial (D5A, D5B)

- **Speed marks:** `1`, `R`, `2` on the speed dial rim at V1, VR, V2 while takeoff mode is on, the next one to reach in yellow (`speedMarks` in `gauges/airspeed.ts`). No `1` off a runway. No numbers are shown for the speeds.
- **V1 merged:** a V1 within 5 kt (`V1_MERGE_KT`) of VR has no mark, and VR's call-out reads `V1 ROTATE`. That is the usual case at default mass on both airports' runways.
- **Call-outs** (`views/takeoffStrip.ts`, drawn by `views/instruments.ts`): large text over the top of the speed dial and horizon, `V1`, `ROTATE` (or `V1 ROTATE`), `POSITIVE RATE` (5 m up, climbing) and `V2`, 2 s each, once per takeoff, in order. `POSITIVE RATE` adds `GEAR UP` while the gear lever is down; `V2` waits for it to end. A separate live region (`#callout-sr`) speaks each one.
- **Power dial** (`gauges/power.ts`): no label on it (the docs and code call its scale N1). White needle and green fill: engine. Yellow marker on the rim: lever. Amber band: the gap between them. Boxed engine value and `THR` value. The red arc from 95 is decoration.
- **Power scale:** the dial, `THR` and the screen-reader line read 20 at idle to 100 at full (`powerPct` in `views/format.ts`). Display only; thrust, V1 and the autopilot are unchanged, and the debug panel still shows 0 to 100.
- **Slow spool from idle** (`spoolUpTau` in `sim/flight/forces.ts`): spooling up, the lag is 14 s at idle (`spoolIdleTauS`, slider "Spool lag at idle") and eases smoothly to the normal 1.2 s at 40% thrust (`SPOOL_FULL_RESPONSE`, 52 on the dial). Full lever from idle reads 40 after about 3 s and 90 after about 5.5 s. Spooling down is unchanged. Applies in the air too, so a late go-around is harder. At 0 on the slider there is no slow range.
- **Stable mark:** a green tick at 40 on the dial (`SPOOL_STABLE`), and a `STABLE` call-out the first time the engine passes it on the ground in takeoff mode. It is not a gate.
- **IDLE:** at minimum power the boxed value reads `IDLE`; the line under it reads `IDLE` when only the lever is at minimum (`powerLabels`). The screen reader says "idle".

## Runway selection (D5C)
